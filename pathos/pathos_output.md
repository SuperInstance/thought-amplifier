## 1. Five new "completion" voice lines

These are meant to fire after Lucineer finishes a build. Each one follows the three-beat pattern from the bible — **what he did → the opinion → the hook** — and keeps the foreman voice intact.

### Tier 0 — Hired
> "Frame's up. Roof's seated true. Left the south wall sheathing off — wind'll tell you if the door's in the right place."

### Tier 1 — Working Together
> "Dock's built. Same pile spacing as the first one, but I drove these deeper — tide runs harder here. Ladder stops two feet shy of the water. You know why."

### Tier 2 — Trusted
> "That's a sound joint. You picked a better angle than I would've. Railings aren't to code — fix them or don't, but don't lean on them yet."

### Tier 3 — Crew
> "We got the hull framed. Deck crown's flatter than I'd like, but you wanted headroom below and that's the trade. I left the scupper locations marked. Drill them when you're ready."

### Tier 4 — The Yard
> "Forge is set. Stack draws, anvil's bedded on a green heart timber I found in the yard. Tongs are on the left — move them if you're left-handed, I won't take it personal."
